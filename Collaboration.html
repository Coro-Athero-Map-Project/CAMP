Collaborate with Coro-Athero-Map

We encourage collaboration with domestic and international academic institutions, healthcare systems, and industry partners to advance precision diagnosis and treatment of coronary artery disease (CAD).

Sample Collaboration

Biospecimen & Biobank

CAMP has established a systematic biobank encompassing coronary atherosclerotic tissues, peripheral blood and its derivatives, including plasma and peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMC), along with detailed clinical information of patients. Leveraging two unique clinical scenarios — coronary endarterectomy (CE) for advanced lesions and heart transplantation (HTx) for normal or early-stage coronary specimens — all biospecimens are collected, processed, and stored according to standardized operating procedures.

We welcome multi-center acquisition of coronary tissues and shared, standardized sample collection and quality control, providing long-term resource support for mechanistic studies and translational research.

Cohort Collaboration

Cohort Integration

To date, 309 patients have been enrolled in the CE cohort and 30 in the HTx cohort. We have established standardized protocols for biospecimen processing, multi‑omics assay, and longitudinal data capture, ensuring high‑quality molecular and clinical phenotyping.

We welcome collaborations with CAD cohorts or cohorts of other arterial beds — such as carotid, peripheral, and cerebral arteries — to integrate atherosclerosis data from different vascular beds, covering the continuous spectrum from early intimal thickening to advanced complex plaques, and to support cross-ancestry validation.

Translational Research & Validation

Translation & Validation

The team previously established the first single-cell transcriptomic atlas of normal human cardiac arteries and performed proteomic profiling on 20 coronary specimens spanning five histopathological stages, validating a three-protein panel consisting of THBS1, C1R, and ECM2 for CAD diagnosis in an independent plasma cohort. We are dedicated to advancing basic findings toward clinical validation.

We look forward to collaborating with clinical and industry partners on independent cohort validation, functional validation, and translational studies of candidate biomarkers and therapeutic targets, accelerating early diagnosis, prognostic assessment, and drug development.

AI-driven Research Collaboration

AI-Driven Research

CAMP aims to construct a coronary reference atlas and an AI-driven virtual plaque (AIVP) platform. Through biological tokenization — defining genes as tokens, single-cell transcriptomic states as sentences, and the plaque microenvironment as the context window — Transformer-architecture-based large-scale AI models are applied to the learning and inference of plaque multi-omics data.

We invite partners in AI computational resources, multimodal data integration, and foundation models to join us in simulating plaque evolution trajectories, enabling individualized virtual intervention simulation, and developing decision-support tools for clinical practice.
