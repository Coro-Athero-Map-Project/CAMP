Coro-Athero-Map Project
Coro-Athero-Map is a comprehensive biobank and research platform for studying coronary atherosclerosis through clinical data and multi-omics profiling.
We provide curated datasets, analytical tools, and research resources to support discoveries in coronary artery disease.
Overall objective
To establish a research platform integrating a coronary tissue biobank, blood biobank, multi-omics data, deep clinical phenotyping, and long-term follow-up, and through an open-access coronary reference atlas and AI-powered analytical tools, to drive the transition of CAD from conventional empirical therapy to molecular subtype-guided precision medicine.

Research Overview

To date, our team has established three complementary coronary artery cohorts that form the foundation of our multi‑omics investigation. The first is a 16‑sample pilot cohort, designed for discovery and workflow optimization. The second is a 233‑sample carotid endarterectomy (CE) cohort, which provides the statistical power and depth necessary for robust molecular profiling. The third is a 92‑sample heart transplantation (HTx) cohort, offering a unique window into the molecular determinants of advanced atherosclerotic disease.

Leveraging this extensive biobank, we are now pioneering the integration of artificial intelligence with drug screening to accelerate therapeutic discovery for coronary atherosclerosis. Under the visionary leadership of Professor Zhan Hu, our team remains at the forefront of cardiovascular multi‑omics research in China, committed to translating scientific discoveries into meaningful clinical impact.
